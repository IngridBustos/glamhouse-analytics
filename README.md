# Glam House Analytics

## Overview

Glam House Analytics is a portfolio project that simulates the design and implementation of a relational database for a beauty salon.

The goal is to manage business operations and generate insights using SQL, PostgreSQL, Python, and Power BI.

---

## Business Problem

Beauty salons manage appointments, customers, employees, services and sales every day.

Without a structured database it is difficult to answer questions such as:

- Which services generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the most loyal customers?
- Which employee performs the most services?
- What are the monthly sales?
- Which promotions are the most successful?

This project aims to solve those business problems through data.
